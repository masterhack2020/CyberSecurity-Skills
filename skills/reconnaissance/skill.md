# Reconnaissance & OSINT

## Overview

Reconnaissance is the process of collecting and analyzing information about authorized targets to understand assets, exposure, and security posture.

## Objectives

- Identify public attack surface
- Understand information exposure
- Support defensive security assessments

## Knowledge Areas

- OSINT principles
- Asset discovery
- Domain intelligence
- Metadata analysis
- Threat intelligence basics

## Defensive Considerations

Organizations should monitor exposed information and reduce unnecessary public disclosure.

## Responsible Use

Only perform reconnaissance against systems where explicit authorization exists.
